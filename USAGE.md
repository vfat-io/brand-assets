# Using VFAT assets

## App integrations and referrals

- Write the brand name as **VFAT**. The primary website is **https://vfat.io**.
- Use the bird for compact UI, the horizontal logo when the name needs to be visible, and the app icon for app directories or referral cards.
- Link to the relevant VFAT page or your existing VFAT referral URL. Preserve any referral parameters supplied to you by VFAT.
- Use accurate labels such as “Open VFAT”, “View on VFAT”, or “Explore VFAT”. Describe an integration or partnership only when that relationship exists.
- Provide an accessible name for links and buttons. A decorative logo beside visible “VFAT” text can use an empty image `alt`.

## Social posts and partner announcements

Use the supplied landscape or square PNG for a ready-to-upload VFAT image. Choose the aspect ratio that fits your post and check the platform preview before publishing. The SVG companions can be placed in a design tool; their lettering is already outlined.

The 1500 × 500 X banner is a profile header. Upload it as supplied: its layout keeps the lower-left corner, where X places the profile photo, clear.

For a joint announcement, place your logo and a supplied VFAT logo in your own layout with clear spacing and similar visual prominence. Keep the VFAT artwork intact. The supplied cards are neutral brand images; add factual announcement copy in your post or surrounding design. You can tag **[@vfat_io](https://x.com/vfat_io)**.

## Legibility and layout

- Use the black logo on light backgrounds and the white logo on dark backgrounds.
- Keep the original proportions. Scale the whole asset uniformly.
- Leave clear space around the logo. As a practical starting point, allow at least one quarter of the bird's height on each side.
- Start at 24 px high for a standalone bird or 120 px wide for a horizontal logo, and check legibility at the actual display size. Use a larger mark where the bird's details become unclear.
- Preserve the full bird, including its tail and feet. For circular masks, use the padded square icon and check the crop.
- Avoid stretching, recoloring, rotating, adding outlines or effects, placing text over the logo, or combining it into a new logo.
- Social images have their own spacing. Keep their original aspect ratio when posting them as supplied.
